For Consumers
How do I buy produce on HarvestLink? ▾
Browse the marketplace, select your products, add them to cart, and proceed to checkout. You can choose from farmers and FPOs near you or across India.
Is the produce really from farmers directly? ▾
Yes. Every farmer and FPO on HarvestLink is verified. You can see the farmer's name, location, and farming practice on each product listing.
How is HarvestLink different from a supermarket? ▾
Traditional supply chains involve multiple layers — agents, wholesalers, retailers — each taking a margin. HarvestLink connects you more directly to the farmer, which can mean fresher produce at fairer prices.
What payment methods are accepted? ▾
This is a demo platform. In a live version, we would support UPI, debit/credit cards, net banking, and cash on delivery where applicable.
Can I return or exchange a product? ▾
Fresh produce returns would be assessed case by case. If produce arrives damaged or of significantly lower quality than listed, our support team would assist with a resolution.
For Farmers & FPOs
How do I join HarvestLink as a farmer? ▾
Click "Join as Farmer" and complete the 4-step registration form. Our team will verify your details and activate your listing within 2-3 working days.
Is there a fee to list produce on the platform? ▾
This is a demo platform. In the full version, we plan to offer a basic free listing tier and a small commission on sales, with no large upfront fees for farmers.
How does demand forecasting help me? ▾
The demand insights dashboard shows upcoming demand trends for your products. This helps you plan how much to grow, when to harvest, and how much to stock — reducing guesswork and waste.
Who handles the logistics and pickup? ▾
HarvestLink coordinates logistics. After an order is confirmed, our logistics partner arranges pickup from your farm or nearest collection point and delivers to the buyer.
Can FPOs (Farmer Producer Organisations) join? ▾
Yes. FPOs can register and list produce on behalf of member farmers. FPO accounts support multiple products and larger volume listings.
For Businesses
Can restaurants and hotels buy in bulk? ▾
Yes. Bulk buyers including restaurants, hotels, retailers, and institutions can place large orders directly with farmers or FPOs. Visit the Businesses page to learn more.
How do I set up a recurring order? ▾
Bulk buyers can request weekly or monthly recurring orders. Once matched with a farmer or FPO, a supply agreement is set up with predictable pricing and delivery frequency.
What is the minimum order for bulk purchases? ▾
Minimum quantities depend on the product and farmer. Typically, bulk orders start from 50 kg for vegetables and 100 kg for grains and pulses.
Can I get a customised supply plan? ▾
Yes. For institutional buyers and larger businesses, we offer a sourcing consultation to match your needs with appropriate farmers or FPOs.
About Delivery
How long does delivery take? ▾
Typical delivery time is 1-3 days depending on your location and the farmer's distance. Same-day or next-day options may be available for nearby farms.
What areas do you currently deliver to? ▾
This is a demo. In the full version, initial delivery zones would cover Gujarat and Maharashtra, with expansion to other states planned. Check back for coverage updates.
How are delivery routes planned? ▾
Our route optimisation system clusters nearby orders and plans the most efficient route. This reduces transport time and cost, and helps minimise food spoilage during transit.
Can I track my order? ▾
Yes. Once your order is picked up from the farm, you'll receive status updates — Confirmed → Preparing → Picked Up → In Transit → Delivered.
About the Marketplace
How are farmers verified? ▾
Our team reviews farmer registrations, verifies land records and identity documents, and conducts spot checks for FPOs before granting Verified status.
Are the prices fixed or negotiable? ▾
Farmers set their own prices. The platform shows listed prices, which consumers can see before ordering. For bulk orders, direct negotiation with the farmer or FPO is possible.
What does "Natural Farming" mean on a product? ▾
Natural farming refers to zero-budget natural farming (ZBNF) methods — using bio-inputs like Jeevamrut, without synthetic chemicals. It's different from certified organic, but uses traditional sustainable practices.
How accurate is the AI demand forecasting? ▾
The demand forecasting on this demo uses simulated data. In the full platform, forecasting would be based on real historical sales, seasonal patterns, and market signals. All demo forecasts are clearly labelled as illustrative.
