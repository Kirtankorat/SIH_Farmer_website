The Process

Simple. Transparent.
End to end.

01

Farmers List

Farmers and FPOs create a profile and showcase available produce — with prices, availability, and farming practices.

02

Buyers Discover

Consumers and businesses browse products directly, filter by location, category, and price — with full transparency.

03

Order Placed

Buyers select quantities and confirm orders. Farmers are notified instantly and can prepare accordingly.

04

Delivered

Logistics coordinates pickup and delivery from farm to buyer with route-optimised transport.
