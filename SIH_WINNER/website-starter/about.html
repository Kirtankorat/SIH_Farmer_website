Our Mission

Making agricultural markets
more direct and fair.

HarvestLink is built to reduce unnecessary friction between farmers and buyers — so more value stays with the people who grow our food.

The Problem

A fragmented market that leaves farmers behind.

Farmers in India often sell produce through multiple intermediaries — each taking a margin — before it reaches the consumer. This system means farmers earn less while consumers pay more.

At the same time, unpredictable demand and poor logistics visibility make planning difficult. Produce spoils, opportunities are missed, and both sides of the market suffer.

These are not new problems. But they're solvable with the right tools and connections.

🧑‍🌾
Farmer earns less
Multiple middlemen reduce the farmer's share of the final price significantly.
🛒
Consumers pay more
Each step in the supply chain adds cost, passed on to the final buyer.
🗓️
Demand is unpredictable
Farmers often don't know how much to grow, leading to oversupply or shortage.
🚛
Logistics are inefficient
Poor route planning leads to delays, food waste, and higher transportation costs.
🛒
Direct Marketplace
Farmers and FPOs list their produce directly. Buyers browse and order without unnecessary intermediaries.
📊
Demand Insights
AI-assisted forecasting helps farmers understand demand before it spikes or drops, so they can plan supply effectively.
🗺️
Route Optimisation
Logistics coordination ensures produce moves efficiently from farm to buyer with minimal delay or waste.
Our Approach

One platform. Three solutions.

HarvestLink is not just a marketplace. It combines direct trading, demand planning tools, and logistics coordination into one connected platform.

We focus on simplicity. Farmers shouldn't need technical expertise to use the platform. And buyers should be able to discover, order, and receive fresh produce without complexity.

The goal is clear: more of every rupee stays with the farmer, and fresh produce reaches the consumer faster and at a lower cost.
